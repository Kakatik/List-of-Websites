How to access .onion links

For starters, you will need a browser configured to use the TOR network, the most basic one is the TOR Browser, it is specifically made for the TOR network. The TOR Browser hides your adress using different servers, and that's why it's so private. TOR Browser isn't super safe by its own, people can still find a way to find your actual IP and other info, so that's why it is most preferable to use a VPN while surfing the dark web (.onion links).

You can also access .onion links through Firefox since the TOR Browser is based off Firefox, but I do not recommend configuring Firefox to access .onion links since it doesn't have the same modifications as the TOR Browser, and it can be pretty risky since Firefox doesn't hide your IP adress with different servers.
